Privacy notice

Version 2026-11-28. Last updated November 28, 2026.

Nevio is a research-stage project building a skin-lesion triage tool for primary care clinics. This notice covers this website and its pilot request form. It is written for the healthcare professionals who use the form.

What we collect

Only what you type into the pilot request form: your name, role, clinic or organization, work email, city and state, and an optional message. We also record when you sent it, which site page it came from, whether you agreed to be contacted about the pilot, and whether you asked for occasional updates.

This website uses no cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or other trackers. To stop abuse, we count how many requests come from one network address in an hour. We count with a scrambled (keyed hash) form of the address, never the address itself, keep it apart from your request, and delete it within a day.

What we do not collect

No patient information. Please don't put any in the form. The Nevio research app reads photos on the clinic's own device, and photos never come to this website.

Why we use it

  • To reply to you about the free research pilot and set up a call.
  • Only if you ticked the box: to send occasional updates about Nevio. You can stop them at any time.

We don't sell or rent your details, and we don't use them for advertising.

Who handles it for us

  • Cloudflare runs the service that receives the form and the database that stores requests, and may check that the form was sent by a person (Cloudflare Turnstile).
  • Resend emails new requests to the Nevio team.
  • FormSubmit is a backup: if our database can't be reached, it emails your request to the team instead.

Only the Nevio team can see requests. Viewing them requires signing in.

How long we keep it

Requests are deleted automatically two years after you send them. If you ask us to delete your details sooner, we will.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or to stop updates, at any time. Reply to any email from us, or send a request through the pilot form and say what you'd like in the message. We aim to act within 30 days.

Changes

If this notice changes, the version and date at the top change with it. Each request records the version that was current when it was sent.
